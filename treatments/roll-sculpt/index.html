Roll Sculpt

Rolling wooden massage

45 min$60

Reserve the rollers

Rows of profiled wooden rollers turn beneath you, glowing with infrared warmth. You lean, sit and lie against them, moving through a sequence of positions that travels around the body.

It is closer to a massage than a machine: rhythmic, firm, and entirely in your hands. You control the speed, and how much of your weight you give it.

Built into the machine

  • Profiled wooden rollers
  • Infrared warmth from within
  • Colour light
  • Red collagen lamp
  • Programmed sessions

The sequence

Six positions, one slow circuit.

  1. 01

    Calves

    Begin low

    Seated, legs resting on the rollers. The gentlest way to start.

  2. 02

    Thighs

    Front and back

    A few minutes each side, leaning in as much as feels good.

  3. 03

    Hips

    Turn sideways

    Shift your weight to change the pressure. Small moves do a lot.

  4. 04

    Waist

    Slow down

    Lying across the rollers, unhurried. The pace drops here.

  5. 05

    Back

    Lean into it

    Seated and leaning back. For many guests, the favourite.

  6. 06

    Arms

    A light finish

    Upper arms and shoulders, to close the circuit.

Before you come

Arrive ready.

  1. 01

    Wear

    Close-fitting sportswear

    Leggings and a fitted top, so fabric glides instead of bunching.

  2. 02

    Before

    Come as you are

    No preparation needed. We guide you through every position the first time.

  3. 03

    After

    Water and a pause

    You will feel loose and a little glowing. Drink some water and enjoy it.

Good to know

Does it hurt?

It should feel firm and good, like a strong massage. You can soften it instantly by leaning less.

Will someone show me what to do?

Yes. We walk you through the sequence on your first visit; after that most guests know it by heart.

How fast do the rollers turn?

As fast or as slow as you like. The speed is yours to set and change.

Is it right for everyone?

If you are pregnant, have a heart condition, a pacemaker, a recent injury or any medical concern, please speak to your doctor before booking, and tell us before you start.
