01/03Warmth

Inside the capsule

Three things happen at once.

  1. 01

    Warmth

    Heat from the lamps

    Infrared warms the lower body while you move, steady and even.

  2. 02

    Pressure

    A soft pull

    The capsule draws the air down slightly. You feel it as lightness around the legs, never as force.

  3. 03

    Pace

    A walk, not a workout

    You pick the speed and can change it from the screen. You should be able to talk the whole time.

Before you come

Arrive ready.

  1. 01

    Wear

    Walking clothes

    Leggings or shorts, clean trainers, and a top you are happy to move in.

  2. 02

    Before

    Arrive unhurried

    Come ten minutes early so we can fit the waist seal comfortably.

  3. 03

    After

    A short stretch

    Legs feel warm and pleasantly worked. A minute of stretching finishes it well.

Good to know

Do I need to be fit?

No. If you can walk for half an hour, you can do this.

What does the vacuum feel like?

A gentle hug at the waist and a light, airy feeling in the legs. Tell us if anything is uncomfortable and we adjust it straight away.

Can I listen to something?

Yes. Bring headphones if you like; your hands are free.

Is it right for everyone?

If you are pregnant, have a heart condition, a pacemaker, a recent injury or any medical concern, please speak to your doctor before booking, and tell us before you start.
