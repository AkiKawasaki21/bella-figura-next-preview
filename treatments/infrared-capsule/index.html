Infrared Capsule

Deep, quiet heat

40 min$65

Reserve the capsule

The capsule is a private cocoon. You lie back on a padded bed, the shell lowers around you, and the room goes quiet.

Infrared lamps warm you directly, the way sunlight does, instead of heating the air. The heat is deep, dry and calm, and you set how strong it is from the screen above you.

Built into the machine

  • Full-spectrum infrared: IR-A, IR-B and IR-C
  • Electric backrest, set to your comfort
  • Sound system
  • Phone and cup holders within reach

Inside the session

From first warmth to cool down.

  1. 01

    Settle

    You lie back

    We set a gentle starting level and show you the controls. The shell comes down and the lamps begin to glow.

  2. 02

    Warm

    The heat arrives

    Within a few minutes the warmth reaches you. Breathing slows. Most guests close their eyes about here.

  3. 03

    Drift

    The long middle

    The quiet part. Play something through the speakers or enjoy the silence. Raise or lower the heat whenever you like.

  4. 04

    Return

    Cool down

    The lamps fade and the shell lifts. Take your time getting up. Water is the best thing to reach for next.

Before you come

Arrive ready.

  1. 01

    Wear

    Something light

    Light, comfortable clothing or a swimsuit. Bring your own towel if you prefer it.

  2. 02

    Before

    Water, not lunch

    Drink water before you come and skip a heavy meal in the hour before.

  3. 03

    After

    Ten slow minutes

    You will feel warm and unhurried. Give yourself a moment before the day starts again.

Good to know

How hot does it get?

You choose. We start low and you can change the level at any point from the screen.

Can I stop early?

Always. You can end the session whenever you want, and we are close by the whole time.

What should I bring?

Water and something light to wear. Everything else is here.

Is it right for everyone?

If you are pregnant, have a heart condition, a pacemaker, a recent injury or any medical concern, please speak to your doctor before booking, and tell us before you start.
