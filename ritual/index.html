Three acts

One visit, in order.

The full experience. Warm up in the capsule, walk in Bodyshape, finish on Rollshape. One unhurried visit with all three machines reserved for you.

  1. IWarmInfrared Capsule · 40 minThe capsule is a private cocoon. You lie back on a padded bed, the shell lowers around you, and the room goes quiet.About this machine
  2. IIMoveBodyshape · 30 minYou step into Bodyshape and the capsule closes softly around your waist. From there down you are inside; from there up you are in the room, hands resting on the bar.About this machine
  3. IIIRollRollshape · 45 minRows of rollers turn beneath you, lit from within by infrared warmth and coloured light. You lean, sit and lie against them, moving through a sequence of positions that travels around the body.About this machine

Booked one by one, the three sessions come to $180. Together, as the ritual, $150.

Good to know

How do I pay?

At the studio, after your session. Nothing is charged when you reserve online.

Can I change my booking?

Yes. Reply to your confirmation email or call us, ideally by the day before.

What if I am running late?

Call us. We will do our best to keep your full session, though the next guest may limit it.

Can I come with a friend?

Of course. Each machine takes one guest at a time, so book sessions side by side on different machines.

Is it right for everyone?

If you are pregnant, have a heart condition, a pacemaker, a recent injury or any medical concern, please speak to your doctor before booking, and tell us before you start.
