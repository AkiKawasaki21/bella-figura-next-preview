One visit, in order.

The full experience. Warm up in the capsule, walk in the vacuum, finish on the rollers. One unhurried visit with all three machines reserved for you.

Good to know

How do I pay?

At the studio, after your session. Nothing is charged when you reserve online.

Can I change my booking?

Yes. Reply to your confirmation email or call us, ideally by the day before.

What if I am running late?

Call us. We will do our best to keep your full session, though the next guest may limit it.

Can I come with a friend?

Of course. Each machine takes one guest at a time, so book sessions side by side on different machines.

Is it right for everyone?

If you are pregnant, have a heart condition, a pacemaker, a recent injury or any medical concern, please speak to your doctor before booking, and tell us before you start.
